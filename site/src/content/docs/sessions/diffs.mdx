Every Edit Claude makes shows in the conversation as a diff, over the code's own highlighting.
A file Claude writes whole shows its size instead.

![DESK-3's full window with two edits as diffs and a written file](../../../assets/shots/sess-diffs.png)

## A diff

A diff sits under its Edit tool line. Its header has the file's path, how many lines it adds and
removes (**+4 −3**), and the view switch: **Auto**, **Unified**, **Split** and **Words**.

The rows are drawn in the code's colors, picked by the file's extension. Removed lines have a red
tint, added lines a green one. Inside a changed line, the words that changed are marked more
strongly.

## The views

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Unified** | The removed lines, then the added ones, one under the other. |
| **Split** | The old lines on the left, the new ones beside them on the right. |
| **Words** | Each edited line once, its old and new words marked in place (`~`). Lines only added or removed show as such. |
| **Auto** | The best view for each block of changes, picked by itself. |

**Auto** is where every diff starts. It picks per block of changed lines:

- Lines only added or only removed: **Unified**.
- Lines edited a little: **Words**, each old line merged with its new one.
- Lines rewritten: **Split** when the session has room for it (the full window), else **Unified**.

<figure class="cut">

![A rewritten block in router.go shown split: the three old lines on the left, the four new ones on the right](../../../assets/shots/sess-diffs-split.png)

<figcaption>A rewritten block: Auto shows it split, old beside new.</figcaption>
</figure>

To see a diff another way, click a view in its header. The choice holds for that diff only.
The **`</>`** before the views opens the file in the card's [workspace](/sessions/workspace/#from-a-diff),
the change selected.

A long diff shows its first 24 rows. **Show all N rows** opens the rest, and **Show less** folds it
again. An Edit with several changes shows them in one diff, with **⋯** between them.

## A written file

When Claude writes a file whole (Write), the conversation doesn't show its text. It shows its name,
its lines and its size: "Wrote limiter.go with 84 lines · 2.3 KB".
