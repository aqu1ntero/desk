---
title: The team view
description: How a card that runs as an agent shows its session as a team, with the lead in the middle, its agents on a ring, and the briefs and results between them.
---

A card can run its session as one of the organization's agents (its **Agent**, set when you
[edit the card](/cards/editing/)). That agent leads: it hands work to other agents as it goes.
In the [full window](/sessions/full-window/), such a card shows its session as a team.

![DESK-3 run by "planner": planner in the middle, Explore, test-writer and reviewer on a ring around it, and the Handoffs column on the right](../../../assets/shots/sess-team.png)

The header shows **Team · \<agent\>** beside the card's status.

## The helm

- **The lead** sits in the middle: the card's agent, with what it's doing (Working, Your turn,
  or "Waiting on \<agent\>" while only its agents work). Click it to read its own conversation.
- **Its agents** sit on a ring around it, one per kind of agent, each in its own color. A node
  shows the agent's name, its model and its state: **Working**, **Waiting** when it's held (see
  [Held subagents](/sessions/subagents/#held-subagents)), **Done · \<time\>**, or how it
  failed. An agent that ran several times shows "×N" and its latest run.
- **The spokes** join each agent to the lead. A spoke is dashed while its agent works, and its
  label says how long the brief has been out.
- **Under or over each agent**: what it's on, or what it found, then its tools, tokens and time.
- **A running shell** of an agent shows as a pill at its side, with its command and time. Click it
  to see its output.

A working node breathes, so you can tell at a glance who is busy. Click an agent to open its
conversation: its brief, its messages and its result. **‹ Session** brings you back.

![The Explore agent's conversation in full: its prompt and its result](../../../assets/shots/sess-team-agent.png)

Agents done for more than 20 minutes leave the ring. A chip at the foot, "N earlier agents", brings
them back, and "Hide N earlier agents" sends them away again.

## Handoffs

The column on the right lists the team's handoffs in order, newest last: each **brief** the lead
gave an agent, and each **result** an agent gave back. Each says when, and who handed what to whom.

<figure class="cut">

![The Handoffs column: planner → Explore, Explore → planner, planner → test-writer, planner → reviewer, then "planner is waiting for test-writer, reviewer…"](../../../assets/shots/sess-team-handoffs.png)

<figcaption>Four handoffs, then who the lead still waits for. Its header counts the agents and the session's cost.</figcaption>
</figure>

Click a handoff to open that agent's conversation.

## Team or Conversation

**Team | Conversation** in the header switches between the team and the lead's own conversation,
with the message box and everything else of a [session](/sessions/panel/).

<figure class="cut">

![The Team | Conversation switch in the header](../../../assets/shots/sess-team-switch.png)

<figcaption>The team view or the lead's conversation.</figcaption>
</figure>

:::note
The team view is only in the full window, and only once the session has started. In the side panel
the card's session shows as a conversation. In a narrow window, the helm shows without the handoffs.
:::
