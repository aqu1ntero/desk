title: Subagents and shells
description: The bar that says what a session's subagents and background shells are doing, the rail with their details and history, and why a subagent is held.
---

Claude can hand part of its work to a subagent, or leave a command running in a background shell.
desk follows both: a bar under the header says what runs, and a rail lists each one with its details.

## The bar under the header

While a session has subagents or background shells, a bar shows under its header. It says what runs
now, in words: "You're running 1 subagent", then each one's name, model and, when it's held, why.

<figure class="cut">

![The bar: three dots, "You're running 1 subagent", "Find how tokens are refre…" and Show details](../../../assets/shots/sess-bar-bar.png)

<figcaption>The bar in a card's panel, while its subagent works.</figcaption>
</figure>

Click **Show details** to see them. From the panel, this opens the session's
[full window](/desk/sessions/full-window/) with the rail. In the full window it opens and closes
the rail (**Hide details**).

When nothing runs any more, the bar says "N finished: history", and a click opens the history.

## The rail

The rail is a column on the right of the full window. **Running now** lists the subagents and
shells that run, and those that ended under a minute ago. Then they move to the history.

![The full window of DESK-3 with the rail open: one subagent, "Find how tokens are refreshed", reading internal/auth/refresh.go](../../../assets/shots/sess-rail.png)

Each subagent is a card:

<figure class="cut">

![A subagent's card: "Find how tokens are refreshed", "Reading internal/auth/refresh.go", "Explore · 2 tools · 9.2k · 8s"](../../../assets/shots/sess-rail-card.png)

<figcaption>A running subagent: its task, what it's on now, and its facts.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **The dot or mark**: a purple dot while it runs, a green check once it completed, a red × if it
  failed or was stopped.
- **The name**: the task Claude gave it.
- **The model** it runs on, as a chip (Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 4.5…), once desk has seen it.
- **What it's on now**, or once it's done, the first line of its result.
- **The facts**: its kind of agent, how many tools it used, its tokens, and how long it has run.

A background shell's card has its command, the last line of its output, who started it ("by the
session", or by a subagent) and its time.

**History** at the rail's foot says how many finished, and opens them all: finished subagents on
the left, shells on the right, newest first.

![The history: SUBAGENTS · 1 with "Find the export's callers", SHELLS · 0](../../../assets/shots/sess-history.png)

The rail's **×** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

## A subagent or shell in full

Click a card in the rail or the history to open it in place of the conversation.

- A subagent shows the prompt Claude gave it, its messages and tool lines, and its result.
- A shell shows its command, what it does (Haiku is asked by itself, see
  [Shell commands explained](/desk/sessions/shell/#shell-commands-explained)) and its output.

![A subagent in full: "‹ Session", "Find how tokens are refreshed", running; its prompt and a Grep tool line](../../../assets/shots/sess-subagent.png)

A subagent that seems quiet says why, as a badge on its card. The bar says it too.

<figcaption><strong>Waiting on &lt;its shell&gt;</strong> (orange): it polls its own background shell, or sits idle while that shell runs.</figcaption>

Each badge says how long it has held.

## When the turn ends first

Claude's turn can end while its subagents or shells still run. The card then stays **working**,
with a status line like "Waiting on its subagent: …" or "Waiting on 4 background tasks: …". When they
finish, Claude takes a turn of its own with their results.

:::note
desk keeps subagents and shells in memory only. After you restart desk, the conversation is all
there, but the rail starts empty.
:::
