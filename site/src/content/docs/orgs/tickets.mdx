Under each organization with a Jira, GitHub or GitLab account, the sidebar has **Tickets**: the
open issues assigned to you in that organization's trackers, grouped by project.

![Acme's tickets: ABC-123, ABC-131 (with its card DESK-3) and ABC-140 under the project ABC](../../../assets/shots/orgs-tickets.png)

The header says how many: *3 tickets in 1 project*. The sidebar row counts them too.

<figure class="cut">

![Three ticket rows: key, title, status, and a link button at the right of each](../../../assets/shots/orgs-tickets-rows.png)

<figcaption>Each ticket: its key, its title, its status, and the button that opens it in the browser.</figcaption>
</figure>

## What's listed

- **Jira**: the open issues assigned to you, through each `[[jira]]` account.
- **GitHub**: the open issues (not pull requests) assigned to you in the repositories of each
  `[[github]]` account's owners, by repository.
- **GitLab**: the open issues assigned to you in each `[[gitlab]]` account's groups, by project.

Only accounts with a token are asked: see [Accounts](/desk/orgs/accounts/). desk reads up to 100
per account. The list refreshes with the links, every 3 minutes, and when you **Reload**.

While desk asks the first time, the view says *Asking the trackers…*; with nothing assigned,
*Nothing assigned to you.* When an account fails, its error shows in red at the top, and the
other accounts' tickets still show. A token in a locked vault says
*Locked: unlock \<vault\> for the rest* (see [Vaults](/desk/orgs/vaults/)).

The search box filters the list by project, key and title, with the same syntax as the board's
[search](/desk/board/search/).

## Open its card, or make one

Click a ticket:

- **It has a card** on the board (one that links it): its key shows on the row, and the click
  opens that card's panel.

  <figure class="cut">

  ![The row of ABC-131, with its status To Do and its card's key DESK-3](../../../assets/shots/orgs-tickets-card.png)

  <figcaption>ABC-131 already has its card, DESK-3.</figcaption>
  </figure>

- **It has none**: the new-card dialog opens in **To do**, with the organization and the ticket's
  link filled in. The card is titled with the ticket's key and title. Say what to do, or use
  **Improve with Claude**, which reads the ticket first.

  <figure class="cut">

  ![The New card dialog in To do, with the pills Acme and https://acme.atlassian.net/browse/ABC-123](../../../assets/shots/orgs-tickets-new-dialog.png)

  <figcaption>Clicking ABC-123, which has no card yet.</figcaption>
  </figure>

The link button at the end of a row opens the ticket in your browser instead.

Once the card exists, its ticket follows it: moving the card to another column moves the ticket
(see [Links](/desk/cards/links/)).
