Every card starts in the new card dialog. You say what to do, in plain words,
and pick the organization its session starts in. desk reads the rest out of what you typed.

![The new card dialog over the board: What to do, the organization to pick, and the footer with Improve with Claude and Create card](../../../assets/shots/cards-new.png)

## Open it

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> on the board, or **New card** in the toolbar. The card goes in the
  selected card's column, or Backlog when none is selected.
- A column's **+**, or **Add card** at its foot. The card goes in that column.
- **New card** or **New card in** _organization_ from the [palette](/board/palette/).

The dialog's header names the column the card goes to. To pick another, see [More fields](#more-fields).

<figure class="cut">

![The dialog's header: New card, the Backlog pill, "Draft kept if you close" and the close button](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-head.png)

<figcaption>The header: the column the card goes to, and the note that a closed dialog keeps your text.</figcaption>
</figure>

The organization starts as the one you're looking at: the sidebar's organization
view, or its Tickets. Otherwise it's the organization of the last card you made.
From a label view, the card starts with that label.

## Make a card

<Steps>

1. Type **what to do** in the box, as it comes. <kbd>Enter</kbd> makes a new line.
2. Pick the **organization**, if desk doesn't have one yet.
3. Optionally, **Improve with Claude** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>): Haiku works your request out into a full brief. See [Improve with Claude](/cards/optimize/).
4. **Create card** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>).

</Steps>

The new card's panel opens and its session starts working right away. Its title,
notes and links go with the first message.

Without Improve, the first line of what you wrote becomes the card's title (without
the smart words below) and the whole text becomes its notes.

## Smart words

As you type, desk picks out words that say something about the card and shows each
one as a pill under the box:

| Type | Sets | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `@Org` | the organization, if one has that name | `@Acme` |
| `#label` | a label | `#infra` |
| `!priority` | the priority: `!low`, `!medium`, `!high` (also `!lo`, `!med`, `!hi`, `!urgent`) | `!high` |
| a day | the due date: `today`, `tomorrow`, a weekday (the next one), `next week`, or a date `2026-11-06` | `friday` |
| a ticket key | a link to that Jira issue | `ABC-123` |
| a URL | a link: a pull request, an issue, a merge request… | `https://github.com/acme-corp/api/pull/612` |

<figure class="cut">

![The pills for "Renew the TLS certificate on the @Acme api servers before friday, ABC-123 !high #infra": Acme, High, Due Fri, Nov 6, infra, ABC-123, and + Folder, links, due, column…](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-tokens-pills.png)

<figcaption>Each smart word becomes a pill.</figcaption>
</figure>

![The dialog with "Renew the TLS certificate on the @Acme api servers before friday, ABC-123 !high #infra" typed and its pills under it](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-tokens.png)

`@Acme`, `#infra` and `!high` only set fields, so they're left out of the title.
Days, keys and URLs stay in it: they read as part of the sentence.

**Meant the word, not the field?** Click its pill. The word goes back to plain text,
and the pill goes away.

A ticket key only becomes a link when the organization has a Jira account for it:
one whose `projects` in `desk.toml` list the key's project, or the organization's
only Jira account. See [Accounts](/orgs/accounts/).

## The organization and folder

A card's session runs in an organization's folder, so the dialog asks for one
until it has it. A pill says **Pick the organization**, and the organization row shows up under it.

<figure class="cut">

![The organization row: the orange Pick the organization pill, the Organization and Folder fields, and the organizations Acme, Globex and Initech to click](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-org.png)

<figcaption>Type to filter, or click an organization.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Organization**: type to filter the list (with fzf's matching), and click one.
  <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the field takes the first match. `@Acme` in your text works too.
- **Folder** (optional): a folder inside the organization. Once you've picked an organization, desk
  shows where the session starts ("Starts in ~/Work/Acme") and the
  organization's subfolders. Type to filter them (`ca` finds `car_platform`, `web/ap` finds
  `api` inside `web`) and <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes the first match, or click one. Once a folder is
  picked its own subfolders stay hidden: type a `/` after it (`car_platform/`) to list them.
  **↑ up** steps out; **change organization** clears both.

<figure class="cut">

![Starts in ~/Work/Acme, change organization, and the subfolders api/ and web/](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-more-place.png)

<figcaption>The session's folder, picked by clicks.</figcaption>
</figure>

Organizations are your folders. See [Organizations](/orgs/organizations/).

## More fields

The last pill, **+ Folder, links, due, column…**, shows every field. **− Fewer fields**
hides them again. A pill that comes from a field, rather than from a word in your text, shows them too when you click it.

![The dialog with every field shown: Organization and Folder, Ticket, the languages, Links, Labels and Due](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-more.png)

The fields, from the top:

- **Organization** and **Folder**, as above.
- **Ticket**: see [below](#the-ticket-switch).
- **Agent**: only when the organization has Claude Code agents. Which one the card's
  session runs as, or **None**. See [Team view](/sessions/team/).
- **Card and ticket in** and **Claude speaks with you in**: the languages the card
  (and its ticket) are written in, and the one Claude uses with you. They start as
  the ones in your [settings](/orgs/settings/).
- **Links**: pull request, merge request or ticket URLs, or Jira keys, one per line.
- **Labels**, comma separated, and **Due** (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next week**, **Clear**).
- **Priority**: None, Low, Medium, High.
- **Column**: where the card goes.

The dialog scrolls when the fields don't fit. Its buttons always show.

![The dialog scrolled down: Links, Labels and Due with Today, Tomorrow, Next week and Clear, Priority and Column](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-more-end.png)

## The Ticket switch

When the organization has somewhere to create tickets (its first Jira project, or
a GitHub account's `repo`), the **Ticket** row offers **Create in _ABC (Jira)_ first**.
With it on, the card's Claude creates the card's ticket before any other work,
assigned to you, and the card links it.

<figure class="cut">

![Ticket: No ticket, and Create in ABC (Jira) first](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-more-ticket.png)

<figcaption>The Ticket switch, for an organization with a Jira project ABC.</figcaption>
</figure>

The switch isn't there when the organization has nowhere to create tickets. See
[Accounts](/orgs/accounts/) for `repo` and `projects`.

## Screenshots

Copy an image and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> in the What to do box. desk saves it next to the board and
puts `![image](path)` in your text. The session reads the image. Improve with Claude keeps it.

desk reads the image from the compositor itself (Hyprland, Sway, other wlroots
compositors, KDE). There's nothing to install. When the clipboard holds text, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> pastes the text.

## The draft

Closing the dialog doesn't lose what you typed. Close it with **Cancel**, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the **×**:
the next new card dialog starts with your text. The header says so: "Draft kept if
you close". Once you create the card, the draft is gone.

## Create card

<figure class="cut">

![The footer: Improve with Claude Ctrl+J, its note, Cancel esc and Create card Ctrl+Enter](../../../assets/shots/cards-new-foot.png)

<figcaption>The dialog's buttons.</figcaption>
</figure>

**Create card** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) makes the card. desk checks a few
things first, and a toast says what's wrong:

- the card needs a title (something in What to do);
- the organization must be one of your organizations;
- the folder must exist inside it;
- a due date must read `YYYY-MM-DD`;
- each link must be a URL, or a Jira key the organization has a Jira account for.
