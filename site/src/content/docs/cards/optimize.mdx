A short request is enough to make a card, but the session does better with a
real brief. **Improve with Claude** turns what you wrote into one: Haiku reads the
organization's code and tickets and writes the card for you to look over. It's optional.

![The new card dialog after Improve: Claude's suggested title and notes (Goal, Context, Steps, Done when) and the fields it filled](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize.png)

## Run it

<Steps>

1. In the [new card dialog](/cards/new-card/), type what to do and pick the organization.
2. Click **Improve with Claude**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>.
3. Watch it work, then look over what it wrote.
4. **Create card**.

</Steps>

<figure class="cut">

![The Improve with Claude button, with its Ctrl+J key](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-before-button.png)

<figcaption>Improve with Claude, in the dialog's footer.</figcaption>
</figure>

It needs an organization and some text in What to do. It works where the session will: in the
Folder you picked, or in the organization's folder when there's none. Without them, a toast says what's missing.

## What it does

The latest Haiku runs as Claude Code in the session's folder (the Folder you picked, else the
organization's), under the organization's Claude account. With a Folder picked, it reviews only
what's in that folder, and the organization's own skills still load. It reads the `CLAUDE.md`
files and the code, and changes nothing. Without a Folder, it may suggest one. It sees the skills, plugins, subagents and MCP tools the
organization's sessions have, and has the session start with the ones that fit.

Tickets and pull requests your request names are read first, through the
organization's own accounts.

While it runs, the dialog shows what it's doing:

![Improve while it runs: "Writing…" with 0:03 elapsed, the stages Tickets, Reads the org and Writes the card, and the title and brief appearing as Haiku writes them](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-live.png)

<figure class="cut">

![The live box: Writing…, 0:03, ✓ Tickets › ✓ Reads the org › ● Writes the card, then "Fix login retry reusing an expired token" and the brief so far](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-live-dialog.png)

<figcaption>The step it's on, the time so far, the stages, and the title and brief as they stream in.</figcaption>

- The stages: **Tickets** (only when your text names some), **Reads the org**, **Writes the card**.
- The title and brief show up as Haiku writes them, so there's something to read before it ends.
- The fields are locked while it works ("What to do · locked while Claude works"),
  and so are **Cancel** and **Create card**. The button turns into **Stop** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>
  again). You can't close the dialog until it ends or you stop it.

If it fails, the dialog says why, and your text is as you left it.

## What it fills

| Field | What Haiku does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | writes a new one |
| **Notes** | your request, worked out into the session's first message: the goal, the context it found, the steps, when it's done, and open questions |
| **Labels** | adds its own, reusing the board's labels where they fit |
| **Links** | adds the tickets and pull requests it found |
| **Priority**, **Folder**, **Due** | sets them only where you left them empty |

A screenshot you pasted stays in the notes.

## Look it over

<figure class="cut">

![The banner "Claude's suggestions: look them over, then Create" with Accept all](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-banner.png)

<figcaption>The banner, with Accept all.</figcaption>
</figure>

The title and notes are marked **suggested**. Your own title shows under the new
one, struck through, so you can compare. Edit anything before you create the card.

<figure class="cut">

![The suggested Title with Use mine, your own struck through under it, and Notes with Edit and Use mine](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-mine.png)

<figcaption>Use mine, for the title and for the notes.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Accept all** removes the suggested marks.
- **Use mine**, beside Title, puts back the title from your own words. Beside Notes, it puts back your request.
- The notes show as they read (Markdown). **Edit** switches to their text, and **Preview** back.

The fields it filled show as pills under the notes, under **Filled in**. Click one to see every field.

## Ask again, or discard

<figure class="cut">

![The footer after Improve: Discard suggestions, Ask again… Ctrl+J, Cancel and Create card](../../../assets/shots/cards-optimize-foot.png)

<figcaption>The footer after Improve.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Ask again…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>) runs it again on what the dialog holds now,
  your edits included.
- **Discard suggestions** goes back to your request as you wrote it, ready to create as it is or to improve again.
