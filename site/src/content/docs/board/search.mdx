The search field in the toolbar filters the cards in the view you're in. Cards
that don't match drop out of their columns as you type.

![The board filtered by 'acme: only DESK-3 and DESK-6 are left, and the column headers read 0/2, 1/2, 1/2 and 0/1](../../../assets/shots/board-search.png)

## Open and close it

- Press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> on the board, or click the field.
- Type. The board filters as you go.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> leaves the field and selects the first card that matches, so the
  arrow keys, <kbd>Enter</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> work on the results right away.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or the **×** at the field's end, clears the search.

The search works inside the view you picked in the [sidebar](/board/sidebar/):
in **Acme** it searches Acme's cards only. It also filters the [Done archive](/board/archive/).

## What it looks in

A card matches when the query matches any of:

- its key (`DESK-12`)
- its title and notes
- its organization
- its last message
- its labels, written with a `#` (`#api`)
- its links: their reference (`PR #604`, `ABC-131`) and their URL

## The syntax

The search uses [fzf](https://github.com/junegunn/fzf)'s extended syntax:

| Type | Matches |
|---|---|
| `bucket region` | cards with both words, letters in order but not necessarily together (fuzzy); words are ANDed |
| `'acme` | the exact text `acme` |
| `^DESK-5` | cards whose text starts with `DESK-5`; a card's text starts with its key |
| `!globex` | cards without `globex` |
| `#api \| #tests` | either one |
| `acme` / `Acme` | all lowercase ignores case; with a capital letter, case counts |

`^` and `$` anchor to the start and the end of everything the card holds, so
`^` is the one that helps: it finds a card by its key.

:::tip
A plain word is fuzzy: its letters only need to come in that order, so `acme` can
also match a card that never says Acme. Put a `'` in front for the exact word.
:::

Some searches that come in handy:

- `#api | #tests`: every card labeled api or tests.
- `'PR #604`: the card that links that pull request.
- `ABC-131`: the card linking that Jira issue.
- `^DESK-5`: one card by its key.
